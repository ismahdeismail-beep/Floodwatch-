# ADR-005: Dedicated Multi-Channel Alert Service

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

Warning delivery is the platform's most critical user-facing function: alerts
must reach citizens within 30 seconds via SMS, WhatsApp, push, email, browser,
and webhooks, with retries, fallbacks, delivery tracking, and expiry semantics.
Coupling this logic inside other services would create tight coupling and make
reliability and auditability difficult.

## Decision

- Implement a dedicated **`alerts` microservice** (port 8007) owning the full
  alert lifecycle: composition, templating, channel fan-out, delivery tracking,
  retry/backoff, channel fallback, expiry/revocation, and webhooks.
- Producers (AI/decision engine) submit alert intents via the gateway; the alerts
  service owns delivery state.
- Channel adapters are pluggable (SMS aggregator, WhatsApp Business API, FCM,
  SMTP, browser push, webhook dispatch).

## Consequences

- Alert delivery is independently scaled and monitored (delivery latency, success
  rate by channel are SLO-tracked).
- A single audit point for who/what/when was broadcast.
- The alerts service becomes the integration surface for telecom and messaging
  partners.
